# App Review notes (Guideline 2.1 - Information Needed, 2026-11-19)

## Kunai Gaiden

1. Screen recording: attached (recorded on iPhone, latest iOS, starting from app launch).
2. Purpose and audience: Kunai Gaiden is a single-player offline action-adventure game for teens and adults (12+) who enjoy ninja / Japanese-themed games. The player explores the village of Aozora, completes missions for villagers, and casts techniques by drawing simple shapes on the screen (V = fireball, Z = thunder blade, circle = sphere). It provides casual entertainment with no ads, no in-app purchases and no account.
3. How to use: launch the app (landscape). Tap to start a new game, customize the ninja, then move with the left joystick, and draw a letter (Z, V, O...) on the seal pad to cast a technique. Missions are shown on the map with a GPS-style guide. No login, no credentials, no sample files needed.
4. External services: none. The game runs entirely on the device (bundled HTML5/JavaScript in a WKWebView). The only network request is an optional load of a Google Fonts typeface; if offline, a system font is used. No analytics, no tracking, no AI services, no payment, no authentication.
5. Regional differences: none. The app works identically in every region. The in-game text is in French (stated in every App Store description).
6. Regulated industry / third-party material: not applicable. All art, characters and content are original.

## Braise Étincelle

1. Screen recording: attached (recorded on iPhone, latest iOS, starting from app launch).
2. Purpose and audience: Braise is a single-player offline prehistoric survival and crafting game for players aged 9+. The player controls Ka in a savanna: gather resources, craft tools, light and keep a fire, hunt, cook, eat and drink, and survive predators at night while completing bloodline journal goals. It provides relaxed exploration and survival entertainment with no ads, no in-app purchases and no account.
3. How to use: launch the app (landscape). Tap to start. Tap the ground to move; use the on-screen buttons to gather, attack and open the workshop (craft tools, light a fire); long-press an inventory item to drop it. Follow the bloodline journal for objectives. No login, no credentials, no sample files needed.
4. External services: none. The game runs entirely on the device (bundled HTML5/JavaScript in a WKWebView). The only network request is an optional load of Google Fonts typefaces; if offline, system fonts are used. No analytics, no tracking, no AI services, no payment, no authentication.
5. Regional differences: none. The app works identically in every region; the interface is available in 30 languages.
6. Regulated industry / third-party material: not applicable. All art, characters and content are original.
